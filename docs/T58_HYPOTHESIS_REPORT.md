# T58 Strategy Testing Plan: results, winning books and bot numbers

Generated by `scripts/run_t58_hypotheses.py`, `scripts/run_t58_bot_replays.py` and `scripts/make_t58_report.py`. Every trade comes from the app's bar engine (`bundle_research.run_trades`), every evaluation and payout from `app.prop.simulator` (Lucid 50K), every governor from `app.bots.risk_governor`, and every bot replay from `app.bots.engine.BotEngine`. Sizing convention: 1x = the strategy's average loss scaled to $500 (1% of the 50K account).

**Splits.** Train 2019-10..2023-12, validation 2024, locked test 2025-01..2026-11. Books were selected on train+validation only; the test window was read after the books were final. A = P(pass inside 60 calendar days, no breach). B = P(pass and reach a first payout inside 365 days); EV is the mean payout less the $172 fee per attempt. NET = net profit and Calmar on an account with no prop rules, size compounding with a drawdown throttle.

### A2 / A3

Dropping the short side of Context Drive changes out-of-sample (2024+) net from $44,091 to $43,733 (0.8% lower): the plan's 25% kill line is not reached, so long-only ships. Adding Range Breakout at 0.5x lowers the A pass rate (62.2% to 49.7%), so it is killed. Trade clocks are UTC: Open Drive enters at 10:30 New York time, which is 15:30 UTC in winter and 14:30 UTC in summer, so a fixed UTC-clock filter mixes two different New York times. The two-stop exit is a combined daily realised-loss cap of 2 x $500 on the book. Open Drive with a two-stop daily exit costs 4.8 points of A pass rate (kill line 3).

## 7. Caveats

- Gate 27 dominates the NET bot profit and most of it is Gold in 2024-26; treat that as a regime, not a promise.
- The books were picked by greedy forward selection on 2019-2024 and then read once on 2025-2026. A pass-rate gain that shows on both windows is credible; the exact percentages are not forecasts.
- The B test window holds roughly 11 months of valid start dates, so the paid-rate columns there carry wide intervals (see the 95% LB columns).
- Gate 27 here covers US500 and Gold only, because no Oil or Dollar-index data was supplied; the monthly signal and sizing follow `app/bots/gate27.py`. It holds positions overnight, so it is only enabled for a net-profit bot on an account with no prop-firm rules (the shipped Net Profit Compounder now runs on a sandbox account for that reason).
- Trade clocks in the research files are UTC; Open Drive enters at 10:30 New York time (15:30 UTC in winter, 14:30 UTC in summer).
- Live feeds need 210 daily sessions for Gate 27; if the broker adapter cannot supply them the Gate 27 legs are skipped with a warning and the intraday legs keep running.
