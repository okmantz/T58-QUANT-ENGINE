# T58 new bots, 2026-11-21: what beat the shipped bots on a sealed holdout

**Bottom line.** The new Payout Hunter (Objective B) beat the old one on the sealed holdout and replaces it (preset rev 5). The new
Evaluation Passer candidate (Objective A) lost on the holdout, so the shipped Evaluation Passer stays. No bot makes a payout within 30 days
of *buying* an evaluation likely: on the holdout that happened on 0 to 5% of start days on every account path. Per attempt, the expected
value is close to break-even, and every 90% interval includes a loss.

## Method (all gates kept)
- Data: repo 1-minute NQ / ES history. DEV = 2020-01-01 .. 2024-12-31 (train 2020-23 split into t1 2020-21 and t2 2022-23, validation 2024).
  HOLDOUT H1 = 2025-01-01 .. 2026-02-28, sealed before any tuning (research/HOLDOUT_SEAL.md), opened once after research/FROZEN_PICKS.md was hashed.
- Trades from the repo engine (scripts/bundle_research.run_trades), real costs, scaled to 1x with a factor from DEV losses only.
- Every account replay uses the repo's own governor (decide_risk), full Lucid rules (EOD trailing $2,000 MLL locking at +$100, DLL, Flex 40%
  evaluation consistency and five $150 payout days, Pro buffer and 40% payout consistency), the bot arbiter (2 open, 6 trades a day).
- Selection on DEV by the minimum over t1 / t2 / validation (A: pass within 30 days; B: first payout within 30 funded days minus 2x breach).
  About 2,300 A and 4,400 B configurations were tried: dev numbers are optimistic, which is why only the holdout decides.
- H1 was in the data the shipped bots were chosen on (Nov 20), so H1 favours the shipped bots.

## What changed in the repo
- `app/bots/presets.py`: Payout Hunter rev 5 = Open Drive NQ x0.25, Ind Rule 15m NQ x2, Open Drive ES x0.25, Compression ORB ES x0.5;
  governor: risk at most 10% of the cushion, day stop 30% of the soft limit, full size funded, stand down once the day already counts
  (win-day lock 1.0x the qualifying day). Account rule set: lucid_flex_50k. PRESET_REV 4 -> 5 (idle copies refresh; running ones are left alone).
- `app/bots/risk_governor.py`: `AccountRulesLite.eval_consistency` (read from the account's best-day rule). During such an evaluation the
  governor aims at max(target, 1.05 x best day / 0.40) and the evaluation is not marked passed until the best day is within the rule.
  Before this, on a Flex evaluation the governor treated $3,000 as done and kept the bot near minimum size (dev pass-in-30 on Flex:
  shipped A 9% before, 28% after; H1: 6% before, 16% after, with more breaches because it keeps trading). Pro has no such rule: unchanged.
- `AccountRulesLite.from_v2` keeps an account's own winning-day payout terms (Flex: five $150 days); `engine.rules_for_account` uses them
  for Objective B instead of the generic five $250 days. Other accounts are unchanged.
- `engine._restore` keeps the new `eval_days` state. Tests: tests/bots/test_new_bots_2026_11_21.py and the preset spec tests updated.

## Tried and dropped
- AQR time-series momentum (1 / 3 / 12-month sign ensemble) as a regime filter on every leg: no consistent gain; trades against the trend
  did as well or better on several legs. Lucid's flat-by-close rule rules out the trend holds themselves.
- Level breakout on the overnight range (new strategy, 48 variants): positive but not significant on dev (t about 2.2), so it is not in a bot.
  File kept in research/strategies.
- New Evaluation Passer book (Ind Rule x2, Context Drive Long ES x0.5, Ind Rule 30m B x1, Open Drive ES x0.5): best on dev (51.9 vs 39.8)
  but lost on H1; two of its legs (Context Drive Long ES, Ind Rule 30m B) were negative there.
