Separation rules. One agent holds one role. A review names the build it grades and its author may not appear anywhere in that build's lineage. The builder never reads reviews. The verifier and the quant never read each other's verdicts before filing their own. A decision to forward-test or promote needs a PASS from the verifier and a PASS (or, for a forward test, MARGINAL) from the quant, from different agents on the same build. The desk never lifts a pipeline verdict.
