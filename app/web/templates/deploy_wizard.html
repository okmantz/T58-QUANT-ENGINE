Deploy a strategy

Four short steps. Nothing trades until the very last button, and every live step shows the safety checks first.

Where should it trade?

Practise first on the T58 demo (simulated money, real prop-firm rules), or connect a real account.

Unlock live deployment

Live trading is locked until three safety switches are on. Each one takes a few seconds.

Connect your account

You are in Demo mode. Real accounts can only be connected in Live mode.

Add a new account{% include '_deploy_connect_panel.html' %}

Set the safety limits

Pick your firm's rules and the limits fill in. T58 halts the day at your daily loss limit and flattens at the drawdown limit, on top of the firm's own.

Review and launch

It is running

Your strategy is live. The Live P&L pill (bottom right, on every page) tracks balance and open trades. You can stop it any time.

Open the live monitor

Practice on the T58 demo

We give you a TradingView webhook URL and the alert message. Paste both into a TradingView alert and the demo account trades along.

  1. In TradingView, open your chart and press Alt+A to create an alert.
  2. Under Notifications tick Webhook URL and paste the URL above.
  3. Paste the alert message into the Message box and save.
  4. Watch trades land on the demo account.
